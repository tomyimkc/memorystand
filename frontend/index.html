3MemoryStand (outcome gate)

Report what actually happened. Memories that decision produced are promoted or demoted based on that outcome — never on a model's opinion of itself.

The trust ladder

unconfirmed

No outcome has been reported for this decision yet.

attested

An outcome was reported, but this deployment could not independently re-check it (no PagerDuty token; a human sign-off has no system of record to re-query).

verified

Re-queried against the external system of record (CloudWatch), which agreed.

disputed

The reported outcome was a rollback, a false positive, or the external system of record disagreed with the claim.

Verification is delayed by design: confirming an outcome moments after a decision often lands on unavailable because the "after" metric window is still in the future — that is expected behaviour, not a broken feature.
